STICKHAVEN

a stick-figure arena RPG · inspired by Stick Online

Move: A/D or ←/→ · Jump: W / ↑ / Space — press again mid-air to double jump · Attack: J or left-click (aim with the mouse) · Special: K or right-click · Shop: B · Chat: T

Every swing and every jump costs stamina — INT raises your pool. Defeat monsters, grab gold and XP gems, level up and spend stat points (STR = damage, VIT = HP, AGI = speed, INT = stamina). Buy better weapons and hats at the shop. Deal at least 15% of a monster's HP to qualify for its drops.

Everyone plays together in one persistent server world — waves keep coming, and if you fall you'll respawn a few seconds later. On phones: a floating joystick appears wherever your thumb lands on the left side (push up to jump), with ATK/SPC buttons at the bottom-right. Turn your phone sideways for fullscreen landscape play.
